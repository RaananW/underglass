Add the latching forms of the Checkbox, the RadioGroup and the SegmentedControl (Instrument, everyday forms), at every presentation. `form="latching"` on a **Checkbox** draws the native checkbox as a small latching square key on a polished bezel with a lit ring (a check printed on the cap when checked, a dash when mixed, 38, 42 or 46 px square); `form="bank"` on a **RadioGroup** draws the group as a preset bank, interlocking latching keys on one polished plinth, one down at a time, each label printed on its cap with an LED above the key that is down; `form="latching"` on a **SegmentedControl** draws its segments the same way. The native input stays the control: it covers its key as an invisible overlay, so presses, arrow keys, Tab, form data and `change` are native, and the key's ring, LED and travel (0.45 of 4 px while checked, all the way while held, 0.2 while mixed) follow `:checked`, `:indeterminate`, the held state (`attachStrataPushButtons`, which now also holds a latching form's key down, and plays the opt-in `click` once per real change) and `:disabled`. `flush`, or no `form`, is the markup and the look they had before, pixel for pixel; any other `form` string is still the native form owner; `colour` is the ring's or the LEDs' role. On the GPU the ring and each bank key's LED are emitters at every level, a bank's plinth is a single part, and every part and shadow stays inside the native input's box (a plinth inside its fieldset's). React, Vue and Astro render identical markup (`form` and `colour` on Checkbox, RadioGroup and SegmentedControl). The RadioGroup's `selector` form is a later change. The push button's CSS now reads its bloom's room from `--_halo` (5 px, as before).
