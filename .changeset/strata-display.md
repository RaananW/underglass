Add the Strata Display (Instrument, wave 2), at every presentation: a glass window in a bezel, backlit, with the text printed under the glass. The editable Display is the TextField with `form="display"` (any other `form` string is still the native form owner): a native `<input>` that covers the whole display, so typing, selection, the caret, IME composition, autofill and form submission are the browser's, and the caret and the selection are drawn under the glass where the text puts them. The read-only Display (`Display` in React and Vue and Astro) is a native `<output>`, a polite live region (`live="off"` for a fast value, `"assertive"` for an urgent one) that is never submitted; a value that must submit is a read-only TextField. `backlight` is a colour role (`glowOn` by default, `accent`, `success`, `warning`, `danger`, `info` or `off`) and `lit={false}` switches the light off; it is also off when the display is disabled or under more contrast. New tokens: `display.bezel.width`, `display.bezel.radius`, `display.finish.band`, `display.finish.horizon` and `emitter.display.radius`, `emitter.display.height`, `emitter.display.strength` (the premium character drops its unprototyped `display.backlight`: the strength is the emitter token's). The recipe has relief and face modes, a focus outline round the bezel, an invalid edge on the window, forced colours, more contrast, reduced transparency (the window shows the plain capture) and reflow at 320 px and 200% text. On the GPU the text stays captured content (the GPU never draws text): Relief draws a groove and a flat window, Objects a slab and a flat window, and Instrument a polished bezel and a glass pane whose flat region is the capture itself, so the text under it is the DOM's own pixels (0 of 30,726 differ at DPR 1) and the light never changes them. The backlight is a few emitters along the window, within the contrast guard over the text (an input's content box and an output's text runs are protected rects, the bezel is not); toggling it is a uniform write, and a changed text is captured once. `driveStrataDisplay` and `releaseStrataDisplay` (from `@underglass/ui/dom`) bound a value written at frame rate to one write, so one capture, an interval (100 ms by default). React, Vue and Astro bindings render identical markup.
