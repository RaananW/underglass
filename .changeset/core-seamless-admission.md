The first GPU admission no longer shows blank frames (#202). Before, `enable()` moved the root into the canvas and hid the ordinary DOM before functional admission, so the output was one black and two to four white frames (about 60-85 ms warm, up to about 0.9 s on heavy Instrument pages) before the first GPU frame. Now the ordinary DOM stays presented, and usable, while the canvas is displayed but invisible (`opacity: 0`, inert) for the functional probe and the composition is prepared (mode `dom` with `initializing`, not `admitting`); a failure there leaves the DOM exactly as it was. Then one task moves the root in, under a held view transition that keeps the already presented DOM frame on screen until the first complete GPU frame is presented, and mode becomes `gpu`. Every frame from `enable()` to `gpu` shows the content, at DPR 1 and 2, headless and headed (browser test with a CDP screencast of every frame, surface page and the heavy `instrument-post-core` page; before, 4-5 blank frames). Your inline `opacity` and `pointer-events` on the canvas are restored; the browser needs `document.startViewTransition` for the held frame (`metrics.admission.cover` is `view-transition`, or `none` when it is missing, the document is not visible or your own view transition is running, which is never interrupted; GPU stays on and only the swap frame can then be blank). `mode` is `admitting` only from the swap until the first GPU frame. New metric `metrics.admission.swapMs` (swap to first GPU frame) and `cover`; `admittingMs` now counts from the start of the probe.
