Add the Strata Meter and Gauge (Instrument, wave 2), at every presentation. A Meter is a native `<meter>` (role `meter`, with `min`, `max`, `low`, `high` and `optimum`) under a bar of LED segments lit from its value, each in its zone's colour by the native rule (`success`, `warning`, `danger`), or, with `form="needle"`, a needle on a printed arc; `peak` holds the highest segment and lets it fall (`meter.peakHold`, `meter.peakDecay`). A Gauge is the same native element under a round dial with a needle, a printed scale over `gauge.sweep` (240°) and a red zone below `low` and above `high` (a bounded measurement, so `<meter>`, not `<progress>`). `unit` or `format` gives `aria-valuetext`. New tokens: `size.meter`, `size.gauge`, `meter.sweep`, `meter.peakHold`, `meter.peakDecay`, `gauge.sweep`, `emitter.segment.*`, and `spring.meter` and `spring.gauge` in the premium character (the CSS carries their `linear()` curves). The recipe has relief and face modes, forced colours, more contrast, reduced motion (the needle moves at once, segments without glow) and reflow at 320 px and 200% text. `attachStrataMeters` (which `attachStrata` and the components attach) keeps the drawing, the zones, the unit's text and a held peak in step with the native meter, and `driveStrataMeter` drives a live value as a uniform and a pose with no DOM write where the GPU draws the meter, so a level at frame rate adds no capture, upload or hit-geometry update, while the native `value` follows at a low rate for assistive technology. On the GPU each lit segment is the Lamp's LED lens with an emitter at every level; a needle meter and a gauge are a window, a needle on its spring and a hub at Instrument (a gauge also a polished bezel), and a well, a bezel and a hub dome at Relief and Objects with the needle as captured content. React, Vue and Astro bindings render identical markup.
