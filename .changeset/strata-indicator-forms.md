Add the indicator forms of the Strata Rating, Badge, ProgressBar and Spinner (Instrument, everyday forms), at every presentation. `form="lamps"` draws a Rating as a row of lamps (a glass lens in each star's place, lit up to the score, a hovered star and the ones before it half lit where hover is real), `form="led"` a Badge as an LED pill (the StatusLight's lens before the text on a dark plate, in the variant's colour; a neutral badge's lens is dark glass, a count keeps its number), `form="meter"` a ProgressBar as a groove of LED segments (`segments`, 20 by default, lit from the value as the Meter's bar is, and a running head while indeterminate) and `form="ring"` a Spinner as a ring of eight LEDs with a head chasing clockwise. The native controls stay the controls (the radios, the `<span>`, the `<progress>`, the status), the accessibility snapshot is the same with and without the form, and `flush`, or no `form`, is the markup and the look they had before, pixel for pixel; on a Rating any other `form` string is still the native form owner. `colour` names the lenses' role. New tokens: `size.rating`, `size.spinner` and the chase tempos `chase.spinner` (900 ms) and `chase.bar` (1,600 ms), never under 334 ms, so no lens flashes more than three times a second; the chase is steady under reduced motion (a comet, every other segment). The recipe has relief and face modes, forced colours, more contrast (flat lenses, emitters off) and reflow at 320 px and 200% text. `attachStrataProgress` (which `attachStrata` and the components attach) keeps a bar's drawn value in step with its native `<progress>`. On the GPU every lens is the Lamp's LED lens with an emitter at every level (a 5-star rating is 5 emitters), read from `--strata-lit` each frame, so a score, a value and a chase are uniform writes: 300 frames of a chasing ring add no capture, upload or hit-geometry update. React, Vue and Astro bindings render identical markup.
