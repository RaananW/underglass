Add the Strata Fader, an Instrument-level component, to the private @underglass/ui package: a native range input, vertical by default (`writing-mode: vertical-lr`, `aria-orientation="vertical"`) or horizontal, over a slot in the faceplate with a printed scale, whose transparent thumb is the drawn carriage, so drag, track clicks and keys are native; `attachStrataFaders` in `/dom` adds the carriage following the value, taps snapping to printed marks, the wheel and the double-click reset. `size.fader` and `fader.snap` tokens, the CSS recipe, React, Vue and Astro components, Relief and Objects presentation on the GPU (the slot a groove, the carriage a raised face or slab), and the fader's Instrument parts and poses for the helper's `instrument` level.
