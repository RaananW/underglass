Add the Strata JogWheel and the PatchPoint and PatchBay (Instrument, wave 3), at every presentation. A JogWheel is a native `<input type="range">` over a large flat wheel with a finger dimple: an endless encoder whose value steps by `step` and, with `wrap` (the default), goes round from `max` to `min`, while the dimple turns on an accumulated angle (`--strata-jog-angle`, a step being `360 / turn` degrees) that never turns back when the value wraps. Keys are native (at an end, the key that would pass it goes round); a circular drag (or, with `drag="vertical"`, the Knob's vertical drag), a tap on the upper or lower half of the wheel (the single-pointer alternative), the mouse wheel and a double-click reset come from `attachStrataJogWheels`, and a flick coasts on `spring.jog` with one native step per `input` event at no more than `jog.maxRate` a second, stopping under reduced motion; `detents` makes the dimple settle on each step and play `tick` where feedback is on. A PatchPoint is a native `<input type="checkbox">` (connected when checked) or, with `button`, a `<button aria-pressed>`, over a jack in a washer: connected, a plug stands out of the hole with a short cable (contained in the box, which does not grow), and `led` adds a StatusLight lens that lights with it; a PatchBay is a `<fieldset>` of PatchPoints on one plinth named by its `legend`; `attachStrataPatchPoints` toggles a button and plays the opt-in `click` (connect) and `thunk` (disconnect). New tokens: `size.jogWheel`, `size.patchPoint`, `jog.turn`, `jog.dragPx`, `jog.fine`, `jog.tapSlop`, `jog.flick`, `jog.maxRate`, and `spring.jog` and `spring.patch` in the premium character. The recipe has relief and face modes, forced colours, more contrast, reduced motion and reflow at 320 px and 200% text. On the GPU the dimple is a primitive at Relief and Objects and a turning part at Instrument (a polished collar, a brushed cap with a polished chamfer, a graphite dish), so a turning frame recaptures nothing (core captures each native `input` event), and the jack is a washer, a hole and a plug at Relief and Objects and, at Instrument, a brushed washer, a polished barrel, a floor, a plug and a rubber cable whose plug rises out of the hole on `spring.patch`, sunk out of sight while unplugged so no state changes the parts. React, Vue and Astro bindings render identical markup.
