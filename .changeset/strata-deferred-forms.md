The three deferred forms of the everyday table, at every presentation. A Slider takes `form="wheel"` (a thumbwheel: the Fader's track and behaviour with a ridged roller that rolls in place under the value, `wheel.sweep` degrees from the minimum to the maximum and `wheel.ridges` ridges round it; at the Instrument level the ridges are the part's own geometry, a polished extruded ridged circle with an ink index tooth turning about its axis, so a turn is a uniform update with no capture). A RadioGroup takes `form="selector"` (the RotarySelector's dial over the group's native radios, one detent per radio with its label printed at its position and the radio an invisible overlay of at least 24 px on the print; up to eight radios of at most twelve characters, any other group asked as a selector is drawn as a `bank`, and `data-strata-form` says which; the dial is `aria-hidden`, the Knob's object at the Instrument level, with a click on the ring, a tap on the cap and the mouse wheel stepping the radios). A Display and a TextField's display take `segments` (the text under the glass in a seven-segment face, `Strata Segments`, with the unlit segments faintly printed behind it: the face is generated from segment data by `shared/segments.ts` and embedded in the stylesheet as a 10 KB data URI, so no font file ships and no third-party font is taken on; characters it has no segments for fall to the code face). `flush`, and an absent `form`, are the controls of before, pixel for pixel. New tokens `wheel.sweep`, `wheel.ridges`, `display.segments.ghost` and `font.segments`.
