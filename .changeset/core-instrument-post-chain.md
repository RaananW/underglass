Draw the Instrument level's shadows and post chain: every `instrument` layer now draws its parts into an offscreen target, runs SMAA and an emitter bloom on it (lenses flagged `emitter` glow while their emitter gives light) and composites the result over its face multiplied by a mask of its objects' `bounds`, so no stage draws outside a control's box or the layer's clip; captured caps are drawn after the chain, so text is never filtered. The scene's `post` flags now draw (`smaa` and `bloom` on by default; `taa`, `contact` and `ssgi` as options), and `shadow.map` draws a PCF shadow map from the key light for parts on parts (4,096, bias 1.95 px, darkness 0.1 by default; `darkness: 1` for none), re-rendered only when parts move; the face never receives it. At a fractional layer opacity the meshes now blend as one group over the layer's already faded face (`α · P + (1 − α) · (α · F + (1 − α) · B)` under an opaque part), so parts no longer show through each other; true group opacity of the whole layer is not drawn yet. Core sets the parts' exposure (0.8) and contrast (1.2) explicitly after the environment loads, the values Babylon Lite's loader set before, so the look does not change. `state().instrument.post` reports each layer's stages (`on`, `off`, `idle`, `failed` with the error), its map and the GPU time per stage, and a layer whose chain cannot be built (its target, mask or composite, or the registration of the chain without its optional stages) is drawn at the Relief level with the new step-down reason `post`; any other build error stays `compile`, and both name the cause. GPU stays on.
