Add the Strata RotarySelector and the PushButton forms (Instrument, wave 2), at every presentation. A **RotarySelector** is a native `<select>` over a printed dial: the Knob's skirt, cap and pointer with one hard detent per option, the options' labels printed round it (numbers on the dial and a numbered legend when it is narrow or the text large, at 320 px and 200%), and the Knob's round behaviour from `attachStrataRotarySelectors`: a vertical drag, a click on the ring or a printed label, a tap on the cap's upper or lower half and the wheel, snapping to the nearest enabled option, with the select's own keys, type-ahead and popup, and `input` then `change` once per option. `form="momentary"` on the **Button** and `form="latching"` on the **ToggleButton** draw the same native button as a machined cap on a polished bezel with a lit ring: the ring lights and the cap goes down while the button is held (`attachStrataPushButtons`, `data-strata-down`), a latching button's cap rests at 0.45 of its 4 px travel while `aria-pressed` and its LED lights; any other `form` string is still the native form owner, and `colour` is the ring's role (as the Lamp's). New tokens: `size.push`, `size.rotary` and `emitter.pushButton.*`; the opt-in `tick` plays as the selector moves to another option and `click` as a push button goes down. On the GPU the ring and a latching button's LED are emitters at every level (a version 4 scene with `emitters`, the protected text and a feather, so a press is a uniform write); Relief draws the bezel and the cap as raised faces and Objects as a slab and a key, the selector the Knob's skirt and cap; at the Instrument level the selector is the Knob's parts turned to the chosen option on `spring.detent` with no capture and no hit-geometry update, and a push button a polished bezel, a glass ring, and a body with a captured cap travelling 4 px on the CSS throw. Every part and shadow stays inside the control's box. React, Vue and Astro render identical markup (`RotarySelector`; `form` and `colour` on Button and ToggleButton).
