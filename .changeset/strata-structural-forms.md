Add the structural forms of the Instrument level's everyday components, at every presentation. A `Slider` takes `form="knob"` or `form="fader"` and draws its own native `<input type="range">` as the Knob's or the Fader's object (the same markup, behaviour and GPU parts, without their printed legend; the Fader's is horizontal), with `marks`, `detents`, `sweep`, `travel`, `unit`, `format` and `resetValue`; `wheel` is reserved for the thumbwheel, which is not built yet, and any other `form` string is still the native form owner. A `Card` and a `Panel` take `form="plate"` (a machined plate: the slab in the `plate` material role with an edge break, and at the Instrument level a rim of parts along its rounded outline) or `form="tray"` (the body, or a wrapper round a Panel's content, is a recessed well with a groove wall of parts); a `Dialog` takes `form="plate"` (the plate rises out of the chassis with the panel motion's eased progress, a uniform update) and a `Drawer` `form="tray"` (the tray slides out of its edge, in CSS and as a GPU layer travel, with its wall rising). `flush`, or no `form`, is the control of before, in markup, look and motion. Every part is inside its element's box over the whole rise (a mesh swept along the outline, inset by the box rule's margin, checked with core's containment), casts no shadow on the face, and keeps text and hits untouched. New tokens: `plate.rim`, `plate.height`, `plate.chamfer`, `plate.wall` and `plate.slope`. The recipe has relief and face modes, forced colours, more contrast, reduced motion and reflow at 320 px and 200% text. React, Vue and Astro bindings render identical markup.
