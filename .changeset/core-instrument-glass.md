Draw glass and the page probe in the `instrument` material. Parts with `glass: "lens"` or `"window"` (validated and left out until now) are drawn by a shader of their own that loads the layer's capture and traces Rendering Model 2D.6: a lens refracts the capture in at its dome and out at its flat bottom, one index per colour channel (`dispersion`), a window shifts the capture against a flat pane, and both add the Fresnel reflection of the studio, of the lens's `stand` and of the face, tinted and absorbed through the glass. Text under a window (inside the scene's `protected` rects) and a window's flat region are the capture texel, kept exact against SMAA by an exact-only twin in the caps target; a lit lens glows through its glass. Glass is drawn in the same target as the other parts (the chain, the clip and the opacity apply), casts on the face like any part unless its `caster` is `null`, and neither casts into nor receives from the shadow map. Every Instrument layer now also holds a page probe unless its scene says `environment.page: { enabled: false }`: a cube texture (512² by default, 16.8 MB) filled on the GPU from the layer's capture and the studio and bound as the specular environment of its PBR parts, so metals and coats reflect the faceplate print and neighbouring controls. It is refreshed when the capture is uploaded again, its texture is replaced or the layer moves, never per frame, and at most four layers per stage hold one. A probe or glass that cannot be built is left out (the studio alone, or the face showing through), diagnosed, GPU on. `state().instrument.layers[]` gains `probe` and `glass`, and `state().instrument` gains `probes`. `finish: true` on a window is the zone with its defaults (band 0.3, horizon 0.25), in core and in the Rendering Model's reference (`@underglass/ui`); the conformance suite's glass checks are now asserted at DPR 1 and 2.
