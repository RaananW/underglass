Add the `playful` character (`characters/playful.json`, a sparse layer over premium: soft-touch rubber caps in sky blue and coral, candy-lacquer skirts, levers, bezels and plates (a mint faceplate by day, grape in the dark), a bright high-key room, hot-pink lamps, bouncy underdamped springs, a fat pointer and a bright, short sound set), selected with `data-strata-character="playful"` like the other characters. The Instrument level now builds a preset's coat from its tokens, so the candy lacquers are coated there. Without the attribute nothing changes.
