Add emitters and the pointer light: version 4 scenes of the `relief`, `objects` and `instrument` materials take `emitters` (point lights in layer px: `position`, `colour`, `strength`, `radius`, `reach: "outset" | "layer"`, `face`), `protected` (the layer's text runs, or `"extent"`) and `feather`. Each lit layer gets a face pool pass right after its own pass that multiplies what is on the canvas by `1 + E` over its box and outset, so a lamp lights its faceplate and the page or panel past its edge; the relief shading adds each emitter's highlight on bevels, object sides and domes; Instrument parts are lit by Babylon Lite clustered lights with the same values. Inside protected rects (or each cell of a protected box) the face's whole change (relief, part shadows and light) stays within `interior.max`, and a layer adds no light to lower layers' text under its outset. A layer draws the 64 emitters with the most reach into it and reports the rest in `state().emitters` (never a validation error). The new `MaterialHandle.setEmitters(emitters)` moves lights every frame (the pointer light) with uniform writes only: no capture, upload, hit-geometry change or rebuild, and core draws every frame a light was written. For `instrument` scenes the format limits change: `emitters` up to 1,024 (was 64), `protected` up to 4,096 rects (was 64; more than 64 protect the whole box), `feather` 0..16 (was 0..1,000). `state().emitters` reports each layer's emitters, drops, guard and face pass, with `emitterGpuMs` measurement samples and `resources.lightPasses`. New exported types: `SceneEmitter` and `ProtectedRects`.
