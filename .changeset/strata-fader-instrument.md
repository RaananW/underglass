Draw the Strata Fader at the GPU helper's `instrument` level: the carriage is the premium fader's chamfered, brushed carriage with its grip, sliding the slot on `spring.fader` (uniform updates, no recapture), bounded by the input's box, with the slot a groove and the scale printed on the face. Its caster is capped where its shadow would leave the box (above 50% depth), and it takes the input's hover and disabled state. A detented fader plays the `tick` feedback as it passes a detent; feedback stays off by default.
