Add the Strata Pad and Keypad (Instrument, wave 3), at every presentation. A `Pad` is a native `<button>`, momentary or (`latching`, `aria-pressed`) a toggle, drawn as a square soft cap lit from below in a `PadGrid` (`<div role="group">`, `columns`): a rim of light under the cap is an emitter (the Lamp's wiring, `--strata-lit`, `colour` a role) and the cap travels on the PushButton's `--strata-push-t` and `spring.button` (3 px held, 0.45 of it latched). The optional press velocity, 0 to 1, is `data-strata-velocity` and a bubbling `strata-pad-velocity` event, from the pointer's pressure where the hardware reports it, else where on the cap the press lands, the keys 1; nothing depends on it. A `Keypad` is a `<fieldset>` (`label` its legend) of `KeypadKey` buttons, machined square caps with a printed legend and an optional smaller second one (`shift`; `shifted` prints it large) on one polished plinth, `columns` wide, each key travelling 2.5 px; it has no light. The pads of a grid and the keys of a keypad are one tab stop (a roving tabindex) and the arrow keys move in both axes by the laid-out columns, Home and End along the row, Control+Home and Control+End to the ends, with no wrap and disabled controls skipped; the controls stay buttons (no `role="grid"`). `attachStrataPads`, `attachStrataKeypads`, `attachStrataPadLatches`, `attachStrataGrids` and `strataGridTarget` (from `@underglass/ui/dom`, attached by `attachStrata` and the bindings) hold a control down (`data-strata-down`), report velocity, play the opt-in `click` feedback and run the grid keys. New tokens: `size.pad`, `size.keypad` and `emitter.pad.radius`, `emitter.pad.height`, `emitter.pad.strength` (and the premium character's `pad` and `keypad` geometry). The recipe has relief and face modes, a focus outline round the glow or the cap, forced colours, more contrast, reduced motion and reflow at 320 px (a 4 by 4 grid of small pads fits) and 200% text. On the GPU Relief draws raised caps and a raised plinth, Objects keys and a slab, and Instrument a glass rim with the pad's emitter, a lacquer body and a captured cap per pad, and a polished plinth object and a body with a captured cap per key; every part lies inside its native control's box and a press is a uniform write with no capture and no hit-geometry update. The soft cap is a satin lacquer, not subsurface scattering: core's recipe has no such layer yet. React, Vue and Astro bindings render identical markup.
