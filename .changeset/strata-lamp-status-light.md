Add the Strata Lamp and StatusLight (Instrument, wave 2), at every presentation. A Lamp is a lens on a bezel over a native `<input type="checkbox" role="switch">` in its label row, or, with `momentary`, a `<button aria-pressed>` with its lens and label inside it, lit while it is held (`attachStrataLamps`); a StatusLight is a small lens that shows a state, named by its `label`: a `<span role="img">`, a `<span>` with its label shown, or a live `<output>` that names changes. `colour` is a role (`glowOn` amber by default, or `accent`, `success`, `warning`, `danger`, `info`); `blink` is a tempo token (`slow`, `normal`, `fast`, never over three flashes a second, off under reduced motion). New tokens: `size.lamp`, `size.statusLight`, `colour.glowOn`, `blink.*` and `emitter.*` (duration tokens are now held to their range too, so a blink faster than 334 ms is refused). The recipe has relief and face modes, forced colours, more contrast, reduced motion and reflow at 320 px and 200% text. On the GPU every visible lens is an emitter at every level: a panel with a lamp or LED takes a version 4 scene with its `emitters` (read each frame from `--strata-lit`), the `protected` rects of its text and a `feather`, so core's face pool lights the page around it within the contrast guard, and a toggle, a press or a blink is a uniform write (`setEmitters`) with no capture or hit-geometry update. Relief draws the lens as a disc and Objects as a dome; Instrument as a polished bezel and a glass lens over the socket printed on the face, with bloom. Past 64 emitters in a layer core draws the 64 with the most reach and reports the rest. React, Vue and Astro bindings render identical markup; `setStrataStatusLight` keeps a light valid in plain DOM; a Lamp plays the opt-in `click` feedback.
