Probe: a pinned stage driven by scroll

Four sections, one viewport each. In ordinary DOM they follow each other down the page. With GPU they share one stage that sticks to the viewport, and scroll position drives the change from one section to the next: a tilt, a custom WGSL materialise and a ripple. See docs/showcase-deck-probe.md.
