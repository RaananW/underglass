## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- 9af1bff: Add emitters and the pointer light: version 4 scenes of the `relief`, `objects` and `instrument` materials take `emitters` (point lights in layer px: `position`, `colour`, `strength`, `radius`, `reach: "outset" | "layer"`, `face`), `protected` (the layer's text runs, or `"extent"`) and `feather`. Each lit layer gets a face pool pass right after its own pass that multiplies what is on the canvas by `1 + E` over its box and outset, so a lamp lights its faceplate and the page or panel past its edge; the relief shading adds each emitter's highlight on bevels, object sides and domes; Instrument parts are lit by Babylon Lite clustered lights with the same values. Inside protected rects (or each cell of a protected box) on a relief face, the face's whole change (relief, part shadows and light) stays within `interior.max`, and a layer adds no light to lower layers' text under its outset; text on bare ground, inside no relief entry, still takes the relief's cast shadows at full strength, as before. A layer draws the 64 emitters with the most reach into it and reports the rest in `state().emitters` (never a validation error). The new `MaterialHandle.setEmitters(emitters)` moves lights every frame (the pointer light) with uniform writes only: no capture, upload, hit-geometry change or rebuild, and core draws every frame a light was written. For `instrument` scenes the format limits change: `emitters` up to 1,024 (was 64), `protected` up to 4,096 rects (was 64; more than 64 protect the whole box), `feather` 0..16 (was 0..1,000: an Instrument scene with a larger feather now fails validation). `state().emitters` reports each layer's emitters, drops, guard and face pass, with `emitterGpuMs` measurement samples and `resources.lightPasses`. New exported types: `SceneEmitter` and `ProtectedRects`.
- 942a333: Ignore mutation records that change nothing: the runtime observes attribute and character-data old values, and a record whose old value equals the node's current value (a same-value `setAttribute`, `style`, `class` or `Text.data` write) no longer invalidates and recaptures its layers, nor requests a policy refresh or reconcile. A batch is ignored only if every record is, so A to B to A and A to B to C still capture; child-list records and added or removed attributes always capture. New metric `state().metrics.mutationsIgnored` and `perLayer[id].mutationsIgnored`. A component that wrote the same value every frame (#165) recaptured every layer every frame; 300 same-value writes now cost 0 captures (299 before).
- 26c2aac: An `input` event no longer queues a capture when its target carries `data-underglass-gpu-value` (`GPU_VALUE_ATTRIBUTE`): the mark of a presenter that draws the value itself and hides the native paint. Elements without it capture on `input` exactly as before, and DOM that a handler writes is still captured by the mutation observer. The mark is a promise that the element's paint and state-keyed CSS do not depend on its value (api.md lists the conditions). Skipped events are counted in `state().metrics.inputCapturesSkipped` and per layer (layers containing the element or contained by it, as `queueCapture` attributes) in `state().metrics.perLayer[id].inputCapturesSkipped`.
- e4075a9: Draw custom parts in the Instrument level: the `instrument` material's scene takes `mesh` profiles (inline triangles, or a reference to a glB/glTF by URL or bytes, with `scale`, `offset` and a declared `bounds`) and the `custom` part kind, with every property of the parametric parts (`material`, `at`, `pose`, `caster`, `emitter`, `state`, and `glass`: the glass shader draws the mesh). A mesh is validated with the path and the reason of the first problem (non-finite values, a degenerate triangle, indices out of range, missing normals unless `"compute"`, more than 16,384 vertices or 32,768 triangles, a declared box that does not hold it) and every triangle is wound against its normals, so no top is lit as if it faced inward. Containment (the box rule over the whole travel) runs on the mesh's own vertices: a part that cannot stay in its box steps its object down to Relief with the `containment` diagnostic naming it, never clipped. A reference is loaded by core, outside any frame, into a per-engine cache under a 32 MiB budget with a small glB reader (positions, normals, `TEXCOORD_0` and indices; no Draco, meshopt, quantization, sparse accessors, external buffers or skins): while it loads, and if it fails, the part is not drawn and every other part of the layer is, no object steps down for the load itself (the real triangles are then checked like any part's, and can push a layer over the triangle budget) and no scene is rebuilt; a custom part may carry a `fallback` of parametric parts that core draws while it loads and after it fails, swapped for the mesh in the frame it arrives; the triangles replace the kind's placeholder mesh in place when they arrive. `state().instrument.layers[].customParts` reports each part as `loading`, `ready` or `failed` with its reason, and `state().instrument.assets` the store. A mesh's face caster is the footprint of its posed convex hull, its height capped so that the shadow stays inside the object's box; custom triangles (the base meshes) over 1,000,000 per layer step the last objects down (`budget`). New modules `instrument-mesh`, `instrument-gltf` and `instrument-assets`; `InstrumentMeshProfile` and `InstrumentMeshSource` are exported types. GPU stays on in every case.
- ac51e29: Draw glass and the page probe in the `instrument` material. Parts with `glass: "lens"` or `"window"` (validated and left out until now) are drawn by a shader of their own that loads the layer's capture and traces Rendering Model 2D.6: a lens refracts the capture in at its dome and out at its flat bottom, one index per colour channel (`dispersion`), a window shifts the capture against a flat pane, and both add the Fresnel reflection of the studio, of the lens's `stand` and of the face, tinted and absorbed through the glass. Text under a window (inside the scene's `protected` rects) and a window's flat region are the capture texel: exact against SMAA for opaque capture texels, which an exact-only twin of the window draws in the caps target; translucent texels (alpha below 1) are drawn by neither shader and follow the face pass (measured within 1 level of the layer without objects, with the default chain on); a lit lens glows through its glass. Glass is drawn in the same target as the other parts (the chain, the clip and the opacity apply), casts on the face like any part unless its `caster` is `null`, and neither casts into nor receives from the shadow map. Every Instrument layer now also holds a page probe unless its scene says `environment.page: { enabled: false }`: a cube texture (512² by default, 16.8 MB) filled on the GPU from the layer's capture and the studio and bound as the specular environment of its PBR parts, so metals and coats reflect the faceplate print and neighbouring controls. It is refreshed at once when the capture is uploaded again or its texture is replaced, and when the layer moves (its placement quantised to a quarter of the cube's texel footprint) at most once per 100 ms while it keeps moving (a resize or a DPR change goes through the same throttle unless it replaces the capture texture), with one more when it settles; never otherwise (poses, emitters and idle frames refresh nothing). At most four layers per stage hold one, and a probe whose refresh throws is dropped by rebuilding the layer without it. A probe or glass that cannot be built is left out (the studio alone, or the face showing through), diagnosed, GPU on. `state().instrument.layers[]` gains `probe` and `glass`, and `state().instrument` gains `probes`. `finish: true` on a window is the zone with its defaults (band 0.3, horizon 0.25), in core and in the Rendering Model's reference (`@underglass/ui`); the conformance suite's glass checks are now asserted at DPR 1 and 2.
- d8e22cc: Add the `instrument` material: `setMaterial(id, { kind: "instrument", scene })` takes a Strata scene in version 4 of the rendering model (`kind: "instrument"`, part 2D) and draws each object's parts (lathe, prism and extrude profiles posed by native state through `t`) as Babylon Lite meshes right after the layer's own pass, lit by the key light and a studio generated in code, with captured caps that keep the layer's labels texel for texel, analytic face shadows, the layer's clip and opacity, and no capture, upload or hit-geometry change for poses. - eb867b1: Draw the Instrument level's shadows and post chain: every `instrument` layer now draws its parts into an offscreen target, runs SMAA and an emitter bloom on it (lenses flagged `emitter` glow while their emitter gives light) and composites the result over its face multiplied by a mask of its objects' `bounds`, so no stage draws outside a control's box or the layer's clip; captured caps are drawn after the chain, so text is never filtered. ## 0.1.0

Baseline development version. Never published; recorded here so the first published release has a
starting point.

- The framework-neutral Underglass runtime: one shared WebGPU engine that presents original HTML
  controls through HTML-in-Canvas capture, while native hit testing, focus and input stay on those
  elements. Ordinary DOM is the fallback whenever the browser lacks the required support.
- An adapter-owned mount boundary that moves one persistent framework root between the page and the
  GPU canvas with `moveBefore`, keeping framework state.
- `CanvasRuntime`, `mountApplicationModule` and `createCanvasHosts`; layer registration and
  original-element enrollment; typed transforms with origins; transitions, coordinated motion,
  `Presence` support and ownership of existing CSS/WAAPI animations; decorative ripple and effect
  chains with application WGSL effects.
- `compileStyle` for typed `Layout` and `Appearance`, returning complete string values any
  framework can bind.
- `./build` (compositor and animation generators for build tooling), `./experimental-worker` and
  `./experimental-worker/thread` (the bounded experimental worker compositor).
